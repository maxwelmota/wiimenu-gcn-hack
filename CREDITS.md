## Community-enhanced features

The modifications in this fork cover analog deadzone, START-to-HOME translation in the Wii System Menu, and non-exclusive Wii Remote / virtual GameCube controller slot selection. The enhanced patch was exercised on a real 4.3U Wii, including dual independent pointers. Hardware testing and reports are community contributions. This does not imply these enhancements originated with upstream.
